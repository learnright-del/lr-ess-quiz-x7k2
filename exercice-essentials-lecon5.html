Countable, Uncountable & Quantités

Complétez avec le bon mot

1I'd like ___ apples, please. (des, comptable)
2I'd like a loaf ___ bread. (of)
3How much ___ the cheese? (is/are, indénombrable)
4How much ___ the apples? (is/are, pluriel)
5I'd like a kilo ___ apples.
6Do you have ___ fresh bread? (any, question)
7I'm ___. (j'ai faim)
8I'm ___. (j'ai soif)
