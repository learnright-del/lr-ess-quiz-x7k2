📄

Structure d'un Rapport C1

4 sections essentielles

Executive Summary : 1 paragraphe — "This report examines... The key finding is that... It is recommended that..."
Introduction : contexte + portée + plan → "The purpose of this report is to... The scope is limited to..."
Body : topic sentence → développement → preuve → lien → "It is worth noting that... This can be attributed to..."
Conclusions & Recommendations : synthèse + actions → "In light of these findings... Priority should be given to..."
🔗

Cohésion Textuelle

Les 4 outils du style C1

Référence anaphorique : "The company failed. This led to losses."
Ellipse : "She can speak French and he can too."
Substitution lexicale : "The problem... This issue... These concerns..."
Connecteurs de cohérence : "In response to this... Notwithstanding... To this end..."
✨

B2 → C1 en pratique

4 piliers du style éditorial

❌ B2"The results were very good. We got many complaints."
✅ C1"The results proved remarkably strong. A considerable volume of complaints was received."
🎯 Nominalisation : "We decided" → "The decision to..." · Passif stratégique : "Errors were made" · Hedging : "The data would appear to suggest..." · Précision : "very" → "remarkably / considerably"
🔧

Phrases Types · Key Sentences

Les essentielles de la leçon 3 Mastery

This report examines the decline in customer retention observed during Q3.
Ce rapport examine la baisse de fidélisation client observée au T3.
It is therefore recommended that a structured follow-up programme be implemented.
Il est donc recommandé qu'un programme de suivi structuré soit mis en place.
Notwithstanding the challenging conditions, significant progress has been made.
Nonobstant les conditions difficiles, des progrès significatifs ont été accomplis.
In light of these findings, the following measures are proposed.
À la lumière de ces résultats, les mesures suivantes sont proposées.
The data would appear to suggest a trend towards increased digitalisation.
Les données sembleraient indiquer une tendance à la digitalisation accrue.
It would be remiss not to acknowledge the team's outstanding contribution.
Il serait négligent de ne pas reconnaître la contribution exceptionnelle de l'équipe.
