Inversions Stylistiques

6 structures clés

Never have I + PP : "Never have I seen such poor planning." (Jamais je n'ai vu une si mauvaise planification)
Not only did... but also : "Not only did he miss the deadline, but he also forgot to notify the team."
Rarely does... : "Rarely does one encounter such a thorough analysis."
No sooner had... than : "No sooner had she arrived than the meeting started."
Seldom have... : "Seldom have we faced such a complex challenge."
Little did... know : "Little did she know that her proposal would transform the company."
🎯

Cleft Sentences Avancées

IT IS... THAT / WHAT... IS + structures de focalisation

It is/was + élément + that/who : "It is the timing that makes this proposal so compelling."
What + sujet + verbe + is : "What concerns me most is the lack of contingency planning."
All that... : "All that remains is to secure final approval."
The very + nom : "The very existence of this team depends on this decision."
Such... that : "Such was the confusion that three meetings were needed to clarify."
It is only when... that : "It is only when we compare the two models that the difference becomes clear."

Phrases Types · Key Sentences

Les essentielles de la leçon 4 Mastery

Never before have I witnessed such dedication from a team.
Jamais auparavant je n'avais été témoin d'un tel dévouement de la part d'une équipe.
Not only did she exceed all targets, but she also mentored three colleagues.
Non seulement elle a dépassé tous ses objectifs, mais elle a aussi encadré trois collègues.
It is precisely this lack of communication that led to the breakdown.
C'est précisément ce manque de communication qui a conduit à l'échec.
Rarely does one encounter such a compelling combination of skills.
On rencontre rarement une combinaison aussi impressionnante de compétences.
What distinguishes this proposal is its remarkable cost-effectiveness.
Ce qui distingue cette proposition, c'est sa rentabilité remarquable.
Such was the impact of his speech that the entire board voted in favour.
Tel fut l'impact de son discours que le conseil vota entièrement en faveur.
