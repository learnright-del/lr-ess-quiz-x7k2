Les Verbes d'Action · Action Verbs

Affirmatif, négatif, interrogatif au passé simple

✅ Affirmatif
Sujet + verbe +ED (réguliers) ou forme irrégulière
Même forme pour tous les sujets !
I/You/He/She/It/We/They + verbe passé
I worked yesterday. · She went to the party. (irrégulier)
❌ Négatif
Sujet + didn't + verbe (base)
Toujours didn't, quel que soit le sujet
Le verbe revient à sa forme de base
I didn't work yesterday. · She didn't go to the party.
❓ Interrogatif
Did + sujet + verbe (base) ?
Toujours Did, quel que soit le sujet
Le verbe revient à sa forme de base
Did you work yesterday? · Did she go to the party?
⚠️ Réguliers / Irréguliers
work→worked · stop→stopped
study→studied · go→went · have→had · eat→ate
Les verbes irréguliers n'ont pas de -ED : ils s'apprennent par cœur.
⚡ Avec didn't et Did, le verbe revient TOUJOURS à sa forme de base, même les irréguliers : She didn't go (pas "didn't went") · Did she go? (pas "Did she went?").
🧍

Le Verbe TO BE au Passé · Was / Were

Affirmatif, négatif, interrogatif

✅ Affirmatif
Sujet + was / were
I/He/She/It was
You/We/They were
I was tired. · They were at home.
❌ Négatif
Sujet + was/were + NOT
I/He/She/It wasn't
You/We/They weren't
I wasn't tired. · They weren't at home.
❓ Interrogatif
Was / Were + sujet ?
Was I/he/she/it...?
Were you/we/they...?
Was she tired? · Were you at home?
⚠️ Jamais de DID
TO BE au passé ne se combine JAMAIS avec did.
Il se suffit à lui-même.
Were you ready? (pas "Did you were ready?")
Erreurs fréquentes à éviter : I didn't went to school. I didn't go to school. Was you at home? Were you at home? Did she went to the party? Did she go to the party?
