Nominalisations Complexes C2

Transformer des propositions entières en groupes nominaux denses

Principe : "the fact that they failed" → "their failure" — la nominalisation compresse l'information
Nominalisation de proposition : "that he resigned" → "his resignation" / "his having resigned"
Nominalisation d'adjectif : "the decision was premature" → "the prematurity of the decision"
Structures denses : "the extent to which" · "the degree to which" · "the fact that"
Double passif : "He was said to have known" · "She is believed to have left"
✍️

Discours Indirect Libre & Participes

Style littéraire et journalistique de niveau C2

Discours indirect libre : "The CEO was confident. The numbers were strong. Surely the board would approve." (= he thought) — mélange narrateur et personnage sans verbe introducteur
Participe présent apposé : "Recognising the urgency, the team worked through the night."
Participe passé apposé : "Exhausted by months of negotiation, she finally accepted the terms."
Absolu nominatif : "All options having been exhausted, a vote was called." — très formel

Phrases Types · Key Sentences

Les essentielles de la leçon 10 Mastery

The extent to which climate change has affected agricultural yields remains contested.
La mesure dans laquelle le changement climatique a affecté les rendements agricoles reste contestée.
Notwithstanding the reservations expressed, the motion was carried unanimously.
Nonobstant les réserves exprimées, la motion a été adoptée à l'unanimité.
Having exhausted all other options, the board had little choice but to divest.
Ayant épuisé toutes les autres options, le conseil n'avait guère d'autre choix que de se désengager.
The decision, taken in haste, was to prove costly in the months that followed.
La décision, prise à la hâte, allait se révéler coûteuse dans les mois suivants.
So profound was the impact of the recession that entire sectors were restructured.
Si profond fut l'impact de la récession que des secteurs entiers furent restructurés.
The fact that so few took heed of the warnings makes the outcome all the more tragic.
Le fait que si peu aient tenu compte des avertissements rend le résultat d'autant plus tragique.
