Transformer en Passif

Complétez avec la bonne forme passive

1They clean the office every day. → The office ___ (clean) every day.
2The manager signed the contract. → The contract ___ (sign) by the manager.
3Someone has cancelled the meeting. → The meeting ___ (cancel).
4They will launch the product next week. → The product ___ (launch) next week.
5You should submit the report. → The report ___ (submit).
6People say the company is expanding. → It ___ (say) that the company is expanding.
7He ___ (believe) to be one of the best engineers.
8I ___ (tell) to finish the report before lunch.

Discours Indirect

Rapportez la phrase correctement

1"Have you finished?" → He asked ___ I had finished.
2"Where do you live?" → She asked where I ___.
3"Close the door." → She told him ___ (close) the door.
4"Don't be late." → She told us ___ (not/be) late.
5"Please send me the file." → He asked me ___ (send) him the file.
6Mark to me: "I'll finish your presentation." → Mark said he ___ (finish) my presentation.
