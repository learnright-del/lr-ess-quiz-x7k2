Former le Comparatif

Complétez avec le comparatif + than

1tall → London is ___ Paris.
2big → This city is ___ mine.
3happy → She looks ___ her sister.
4interesting → English is ___ I thought.
5old → My brother is ___ me.
6hot → Today is ___ yesterday.
7easy → This exercise is ___ the last one.
8expensive → This restaurant is ___ that one.

Superlatifs, AS...AS & Irréguliers

Complétez ou traduisez

1fast → She is ___ runner in the team. (superlatif)
2intelligent → He is ___ student in the class. (superlatif)
3Traduis avec AS...AS : "Marc est aussi grand que son père."
4Comparatif de "good" (irrégulier)
5Superlatif de "bad" (irrégulier)
6Comparatif de "far" (irrégulier)
7Traduis : "Londres est plus grande que Paris."
8This film is ___ (good) than the last one.
