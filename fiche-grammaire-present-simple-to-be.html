Les Verbes d'Action · Action Verbs

Affirmatif, négatif, interrogatif au présent simple

✅ Affirmatif
Sujet + verbe (+S à la 3e pers. du sing.)
I / You / We / They + verbe
He / She / It + verbe+S
I play tennis. · She plays tennis.
❌ Négatif
Sujet + don't / doesn't + verbe (base)
I / You / We / They + don't + verbe
He / She / It + doesn't + verbe
I don't play tennis. · She doesn't play tennis.
❓ Interrogatif
Do / Does + sujet + verbe (base) ?
Do + I/you/we/they + verbe ?
Does + he/she/it + verbe ?
Do you play tennis? · Does she play tennis?
⚠️ Règle du -S
play→plays · watch→watches
study→studies · have→has
+es après -s/-sh/-ch/-x/-o · consonne+y → -ies
⚡ Avec don't / doesn't et Do / Does, le verbe revient TOUJOURS à sa forme de base, sans -S : She doesn't like coffee (pas "likes") · Does he play tennis? (pas "plays").
🧍

Le Verbe TO BE · Être

Affirmatif, négatif, interrogatif

✅ Affirmatif
Sujet + am / is / are
I am (I'm)
You/We/They are (You're)
He/She/It is (He's)
I am happy. · They are tired. · He is hungry.
❌ Négatif
Sujet + am/is/are + NOT
I am not (I'm not)
You/We/They are not (aren't)
He/She/It is not (isn't)
I'm not hungry. · They aren't ready. · She isn't here.
❓ Interrogatif
Am / Is / Are + sujet ?
Am I...? Are you/we/they...?
Is he/she/it...?
Are you ready? · Is she tired?
⚠️ Jamais de DO/DOES
TO BE ne se combine JAMAIS avec do/does.
Il se suffit à lui-même.
Are you ready? (pas "Do you are ready?")
Erreurs fréquentes à éviter : Do you are happy? Are you happy? She don't is tired. She isn't tired. I don't am ready. I'm not ready.

2I ___ (like) chocolate.

4They ___ (not/work) on Sundays.

6My brother ___ (study) French.
7The dog ___ (not/eat) vegetables.
