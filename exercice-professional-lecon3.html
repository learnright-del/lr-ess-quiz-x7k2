Modaux & Modaux Passés (+ PP)

Complétez avec la bonne forme

1You ___ call the client before Friday. (devrais)
2She ___ share this. (ne doit pas, interdiction)
3I ___ called — sorry! (regret, j’aurais dû)
4She ___ sent that email. (elle n’aurait pas dû)
5She ___ missed the train. (déduction logique sur le passé, elle a dû)
6We ___ avoided this! (possibilité passée non réalisée, nous aurions pu)
7You ___ come. (tu n’es pas obligé de)
8I will ___ help you tomorrow. (être capable de, futur)

5We have ___ meetings this week. (beaucoup, affirmatif)
6I have ___ questions. (quelques, dénombrable)

Subject: A few tips before your first day

Hi Sam,

Welcome to the team! Here's some useful information before Monday.

You should arrive 10 minutes earlier on your first day — it always makes a good impression. You ought to bring a notebook too, because the morning meetings move fast.

You'd better confirm your parking badge with HR before Monday, or you won't be able to park on site.

You must wear your badge at all times in the building — it's a safety rule. However, you don't have to wear a suit — smart casual is perfectly fine here.

See you soon,
Léa
