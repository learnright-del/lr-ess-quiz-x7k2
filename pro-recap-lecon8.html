Phrases Clés en Réunion

6 situations essentielles

🚀 Ouvrir
Shall we get started?
Let's get down to business.
The first item on the agenda is...
✋ Intervenir
If I may, I'd like to add...
Could I just come in here?
Going back to what you said...
🤔 Accord / Désaccord
I completely agree with that.
I see your point, but...
With all due respect...
🔄 Clarifier
Could you clarify what you mean?
So what you're saying is...?
Just to make sure I understand...
⏸️ Gérer les interruptions
Can we come back to that later?
Let's park that for now.
If you'll let me finish...
🏁 Conclure
To sum up what we've discussed...
The next steps are...
I think we've covered everything.
🗂️

Structure d'une Présentation

5 étapes · Tell them × 3

1
Introduction
Good morning. I'll be talking to you today about... My presentation will take 15 minutes.
2
Annonce du plan
I've divided my presentation into three parts. First... Then... Finally...
3
Développement + transitions
Moving on to... · This brings me to... · As you can see from this slide...
4
Gérer les questions
That's a great question. I'll come back to that. · I'm afraid I don't have that data to hand.
5
Conclusion
To sum up... · The key takeaways are... · Thank you. I'm now open to questions.

Phrases Types · Key Sentences

Les essentielles de la leçon 8

If I may, I'd like to add something to that point.
Si vous permettez, j'aimerais ajouter quelque chose à ce point.
I see your point, but I'm not entirely convinced we should proceed.
Je comprends votre point, mais je ne suis pas entièrement convaincu(e) que nous devrions aller de l'avant.
Sales rose sharply in Q3, reaching a peak of 2.5 million.
Les ventes ont fortement augmenté au T3, atteignant un pic de 2,5 millions.
Moving on to the next point, I'd like to draw your attention to...
Passons au point suivant, j'aimerais attirer votre attention sur...
That's a great question. I'll come back to that at the end of the presentation.
C'est une excellente question. J'y reviendrai à la fin de la présentation.
To sum up, the key takeaways are: improved efficiency, lower costs and higher customer satisfaction.
Pour résumer, les points clés sont : efficacité améliorée, coûts réduits et satisfaction client plus élevée.
